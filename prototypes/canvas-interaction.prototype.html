Controls

Click a Pin to start a Wire, click empty space to add a corner, click another Pin to finish. Space flips the bend, Backspace removes the last corner, Esc cancels. Drag a Wire segment to slide it. Scroll to zoom, drag the background to pan. Double-click a Block to label it. A dark Pin has more than one Wire: click it to start another. Del, Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y work.
